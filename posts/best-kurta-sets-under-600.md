## How we picked

We ordered the five most-sold kurta sets under ₹600, washed each three times, and checked stitching, colour fastness and fit against the size charts.

## Top pick: Cotton printed set with palazzo

The only true cotton set in the batch. Colours held, threads stayed in place, and the palazzo has real pockets. Our tester is 5'4" and the M fit exactly as charted.

## Runner-up: Floor-length anarkali

Great drape for the price, but size **up** — the rayon runs one size small through the shoulders.

## What to avoid at this price

Sets that photograph like cotton but arrive as 100% polyester "rayon blend". Check the fabric field in the listing, not just the photos.
