## Why we tested this

Budget calling watches all promise the same thing. We bought this one at its Meesho price and lived with it for two weeks — office days, workouts, and a full day of calls.

## Calling: the headline feature, and it works

Dialing from the wrist takes a couple of taps, and the mic picks up voice clearly indoors. On a busy road, callers said we sounded "a bit distant" but understandable. Speaker volume is fine for quiet rooms, not for traffic.

## Battery

With continuous heart-rate and two calls a day, we got **4 days** consistently. Without calling, stretch that to 6–7. The magnetic charger tops it up in about 90 minutes.

## The strap quirk

The quick-release pins are extremely tight out of the box. Warm the strap slightly with your fingers before your first swap — you'll avoid scratching the lugs.

## Should you buy?

Under ₹1,500, this is the best calling watch we've tested this quarter. If you only need fitness tracking, the cheaper bands still win on accuracy.
