About Utility Store

A curated storefront + review blog. Zero inventory, zero payment handling.

What is this site?

Utility Store is a curated catalog of products from Meesho and Amazon.in, combined with honest, hands-on reviews. We pick, compare and price-check — when you're ready to buy, you click through to the store and checkout, payment, delivery and returns are handled entirely by the store.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. This never affects our picks or our verdict scores — bad products get bad reviews.

Privacy

We don't run accounts, don't ask for personal data, and don't use tracking cookies. Your buy list and product clicks are stored only in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device. outbound link clicks are counted locally to improve the catalog.

For editors

Site content is managed through a private admin dashboard at /admin. Every change is version-controlled in our public repository.
